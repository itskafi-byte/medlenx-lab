Interactive Scan Guide

Optimal camera alignment, lighting, and cursive handwriting for maximum vision-AI confidence.

1. Surface

Lay the Rx flat. Avoid folded pads and wrinkled thermal paper.

2. Align

All four corners visible. Portrait for Bengali pads, landscape for hospital sheets.

3. Light

Even daylight. No flash glare on laminated pads. Shadow-free medicine block.

4. Cursive

Move closer to the Rx lines. Use contrast toggle after capture for faint ink.

5. Capture

Tap Open Camera (not Choose File) on phones. Hold still 1s. Review confidence badges.

Error Escalation System

Report a misidentified drug or a novel handwritten variation. It is queued for the backend training set — you can also flag from any medicine card after a scan.

BMDC & DGDA Reference Manual

  • BMDC number format: letter prefix + hyphen + digits, e.g. A-12345. A = MBBS / allopathic. Always verify the printed letter matches the register.
  • Verification statuses: green >85% auto-accept; 70–85% review; <70% manual flag required before save.
  • Qualifications: MBBS, FCPS, MD, MS, MCPS are the common printed strings. Extra fellowships sit on the second line of the pad.
  • DGDA: every marketed brand in the 25K index is a DGDA-registered SKU via Medex. Unmatched handwriting is never invented as a company.
  • PII: patient name/age/phone are masked per BMDC compliance. Do not type them into notes.
